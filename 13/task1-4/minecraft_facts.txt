46. There are two hidden Minecraft blocks. They can only be found from commands.

      Barrier Blocks are invisible blocks that cannot be mined or entered. You can mine them in Creative Mode, though. (Use the /give @p barrier <quantity> command to get a Barrier Block. Replace the <quantity> with the amount you need.)
